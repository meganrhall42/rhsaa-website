RHS Veteran Memorial Dedication Ceremony

Monday, May 25th, 2026 | 11:00am-12:00pm
RHS Memorial Garden (in front of the school, close to the corner of N Ida Ave & N Sky St)

The RHSAA invites the community to this ceremony to formally dedicate the "Fallen Alumni" memorial, which was placed on campus in 2025. The short ceremony will honor each of the Roosevelt alumni who were killed as a result of their military service, including a reading of the names engraved on the memorial. The event is supported by local veteran groups, and will include a few speeches.

Note: Seating will be limited and reserved for those with mobility needs. Due to the holiday, the school will not be accessible and restrooms will not be available

Learn more about the memorials and the garden on this History page.

2026 All-Class Picnic

Saturday, July 18th, 2026
Columbia Park (N Chautauqua Blvd)

We had a truly fantastic time at the 2025 All-Class Picnic and are already thinking about next summer! Save the date now.

This is a laid-back, casual gathering of alumni of all ages. Enjoy the sun filtering through the trees, and run into tons of folks you haven't seen in years.
