Baseball Opening Day

Tuesday, March 18th, 2025
Roosevelt High School Baseball Field

Come celebrate the history of spring sports teams at the opening game on March 18th! We will be unveiling banners to commemorate our State and PIL Championship teams in both Baseball and Softball. Event includes alumni sharing comments on this history, and a special alumni first pitch.
Admission is free. Bleachers seating is limited, bring camp chairs if possible. The team takes good care of the field to protect it from water, but in the case of a day-of rainout we will reschedule.
Want to support the teams as they start the season? Bring brand new baseballs or softballs with you to the game to donate. With Portland's rain, balls get waterlogged quickly, and a big, fresh supply will help keep them going all season long.
