2026 All-Class Picnic

Saturday, July 18th, 2026
Columbia Park (N Chautauqua Blvd)

Join the Roosevelt High School Alumni Association and your fellow classmates and let's have a picnic! This is a laid-back, casual gathering of alumni of all ages. Last year we had nearly 400 attend representing the 1950s up to a few 2020s grads.

All alumni and their families are invited. Drop by for 20 minutes or stay the whole 4 hours. We have multiple sites reserved, look for us under the shelter in picnic area "E" or in the adjacent "G" & "H". Table seating is limited but we have a lot of open space, bring a chair and circle up with classmates.

We will be serving fried chicken and everything else is potluck. Please bring a dish to share and a drink for yourself. Alcohol is permitted, but no glass bottles.
