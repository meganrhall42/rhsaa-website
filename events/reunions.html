Your high school reunion is a great time to reconnect with those you grew up with. To learn where people led their lives, the interesting achievements everyone has made, and have a laugh over fond memories. Many of us may have social media now, but nothing beats getting together in person and sharing a smile face to face.

Class of 1975

July 18th, 2025

Here is the result of the planning team's brainstorming session.

Begin the festivities with a mid afternoon tour of the remodeled Roosevelt building. Following the tour we enjoy a bougie, elevated happy hour (4:00 -7:00 pm) with over the top appetizers and snacks at a super cool venue. We are checking into some historic sites in North Portland and exploring catering options. We plan to spend all of our reunion dollars in St. Johns/North Portland.

Exact details are in progress, check out the Facebook group to stay up to date or email "roughriders75reunion@gmail.com".

Class of 2015

June 7th, 2025

This is a tentative date and location. The class is trying to get an idea of how many people would attend. This will be a ticketed event, estimated cost $30/person. Spouses are welcomed but no children. Happy Hour style food and drinks served. Music & photography as well. Would love to do a slideshow with photos & videos if people will send them!

If you have food suggestions, or a new date suggestions please let Lillie know. If you own a business and would like to sponsor or donate to the event, let her know!
