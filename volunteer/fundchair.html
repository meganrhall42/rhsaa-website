Fundraising Committee Chair

Time Commitment of approximately 12 hours/month.

Participation will primarily virtual and over email, with occasional in-person meetings.

We are seeking 1 leader for this role.

This leader should have experience in finance, event planning, management, communications, or another related field, gained through professional, volunteer, or community work.

The Fundraising Committee helps ensure the RHSAA has the resources needed to support its programs, projects, and long-term goals. This work focuses on setting clear fundraising priorities, coordinating efforts across the organization, and following through on plans that align with the Association’s mission. Strong leadership in this role helps turn fundraising ideas into sustained support for the school and its community.

Duties & Responsibilities

Lead the development of a fundraising plan and present it within the first six months of the formation of the Committee.
Coordinate onboarding of new fundraising volunteers.
Host Fundraising Committee meetings at least quarterly, with additional meetings held as needed based on committee needs.
Communicate with other committees to identify fundraising needs and help shape fundraising priorities accordingly.
Help keep fundraising efforts moving by coordinating tasks, timelines, and follow-up with committee members.
Attend Executive Committee meetings held on even-numbered months to support discussion of the Fundraising Committee.

Interested in volunteering? Send an email to our Association President at president@rooseveltalumni.org with the subject line "Fundraising Committee Chair" to learn more.
