Fundraising Committee Member

Time commitment 4-6 hours/month.

Participation will primarily virtual and over email, with occasional in-person meetings.

We are seeking 4 volunteers for this role.

Volunteers should have an interest in finance, but no specific experience in required.

Fundraising Committee Members play an important role in supporting the Association’s fundraising efforts by contributing ideas, participating in initiatives, and helping carry plans forward. This is a collaborative role focused on working alongside other volunteers to support projects that fund RHSAA programs and priorities. Committee members help ensure fundraising efforts are thoughtful, achievable, and connected to the needs of the organization.

Duties & Responsibilities

Participate in at least two fundraising efforts each year.
Efforts may include in-person events, calling campaigns, online drives, or other fundraising activities, depending on committee priorities.
Contribute feedback and reflections from fundraising efforts to help the committee evaluate what worked and what could be improved.
Engage actively in fundraising planning discussions, helping shape ideas and priorities through shared input and discussion.
Attend all Fundraising Committee meetings - at least once per quarter.
Meetings may be held in person or virtually, depending on participant needs.

Interested in volunteering? Send an email to our Executive Committee at info@rooseveltalumni.org with the subject line "Fundraising Committee Member" to learn more.
We can't wait to have you join us!
